Privacy Policy

SmartCal · last updated 19 September 2026

SmartCal is a personal dual-timezone calendar. This page explains what it stores, where it stores it, and what it does — and does not do — with your Google account.

What SmartCal stores

  • Your email address, used to sign you in and nothing else.
  • The events you create: title, notes, place, video link, start and end instant, timezone.
  • Your settings: where you are, your second timezone, office hours, what you called your four categories, reminder lead time.
  • If you connect a calendar feed, the events imported from it.
  • If you turn on reminders, a push subscription for each browser you enabled.

Everything is stored in a Postgres database hosted by Supabase, protected by row-level security: each account can only read and write its own rows.

Your Google data

SmartCal cannot read your Google Calendar. When you connect Google, it asks for a single permission — calendar.app.created — which lets an app create its own secondary calendars and manage events inside them. It grants no access to your main calendar, or to any calendar that existed before.

SmartCal creates one calendar in your Google account, named SmartCal, and writes copies of your SmartCal events into it. Nothing is ever read back: the sync goes one way only.

SmartCal's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the calendar mirroring you asked for. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train AI or machine-learning models, and never shared with anyone else.

What is never done

  • Your data is not sold or rented, to anyone, ever.
  • There is no advertising and no advertising tracking.
  • There are no analytics scripts and no third-party trackers.
  • Event titles are never shown on the public booking link — visitors see busy blocks only.

Sharing, when you ask for it

Two features share data, and only because you choose to: a public booking link, which exposes busy time slots without titles or details; and shared calendars, where you give a named person access at a level you pick. You can revoke either at any time from Settings.

Disconnecting and deleting

  • Disconnect Google in Settings: SmartCal forgets your Google credentials immediately. The SmartCal calendar and the events already copied stay in your Google account, yours to keep or delete.
  • You can also revoke access from your Google account permissions.
  • To delete your SmartCal account and everything in it, write to the address below. Deleting the account deletes its events, settings, sharing links and push subscriptions.
